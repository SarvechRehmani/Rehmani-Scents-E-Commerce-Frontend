Return & Exchange Policy

How exchanges work, what qualifies, and what does not — including hygiene rules for opened fragrances.

Last updated: 1 December 2024 Demo content for a fictional storefront

On This Page

    Demo policy. This document is illustrative content for a fictional storefront. It does not represent a legally reviewed return policy and should be edited before any real-world use.

    Overview

    Because fragrance is a personal care product, the rules around returns and exchanges are stricter than for other categories. This page explains the seven-day exchange window, what qualifies, and what does not.

    The 7-Day Window

    Unopened, sealed items may be exchanged within 7 days of delivery. The window begins on the day the parcel is delivered to your address.

    To be eligible, please ensure all of the following are true:

    • The bottle is unopened with the original seal intact
    • The original packaging and any inserts are included
    • The item has not been damaged, used, or altered
    • You can provide your order reference or proof of purchase

    What Qualifies

    We accept exchanges for the following reasons:

    • Wrong item dispatched. We sent something different from what you ordered
    • Damaged in transit. The parcel or bottle arrived damaged
    • Manufacturing defect. The seal, cap, or pump was faulty on arrival
    • Change of mind (unopened only). You would like a different fragrance and the item is still sealed

    What Does Not Qualify

    Because fragrance is a hygiene-sensitive product, the following are not eligible for return or exchange:

    • Opened or partially used bottles, regardless of how much remains
    • Items where the seal has been broken, even if the bottle is full
    • Products damaged by the customer after delivery
    • Items returned more than 7 days after delivery
    • Items without original packaging or order reference

    How To Start An Exchange

    Contact support within 7 days of delivery. Include the following in your message:

    1. Your order reference number
    2. The name of the item you want to exchange
    3. The reason for the exchange
    4. Photographs of the item and packaging if it was damaged on arrival

    Once your request is reviewed, we will provide the next steps. On a real storefront, this would include pickup instructions and a replacement dispatch date. On this demo storefront, no actual pickup or dispatch occurs.

    Refunds

    On this demo storefront, no monetary refunds are issued because no payments are processed. In a real store, refunds would normally be issued to the original payment method within 5–10 working days of the returned item being received and checked.

    Cash on Delivery refunds are typically made through bank transfer, since there is no card on file.

    Exchanges

    Exchanges for a different fragrance, size, or gift set are subject to stock availability. If the item you would like is out of stock, you can choose between a store credit (in a real store) or a full refund.

    If the exchange item is more expensive, the difference is payable. If it is less expensive, the difference is refunded or credited.

    Damaged Deliveries

    If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, please take photographs before opening it. This greatly helps any claim process. Report the damage within 48 hours of delivery so we can log it correctly.

    Contact

    For any return or exchange question, reach us via the Contact page or WhatsApp.

Privacy Policy

What data this storefront actually stores, where it is stored, and what it never does with your information.

Last updated: 1 December 2024 Demo content for a fictional storefront

On This Page

    Demo policy. This document is illustrative content for a fictional storefront. It does not represent a legally reviewed privacy policy and should be edited before any real-world use.

    Overview

    Rehmani Scents is a frontend-only demo storefront. This means there is no server, no backend database, and no third-party analytics tracking on this site. All data created while using the store is stored only in your own browser.

    What Data We Store

    The following information is saved to your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device:

    • Cart contents. Products, sizes, quantities, applied discount codes
    • Wishlist. Product IDs you have marked with the heart icon
    • Theme preference. Light or dark mode choice
    • Orders. Any order you place during a demo checkout, including name, email, phone and address you enter
    • Contact submissions. Anything you submit through the contact form
    • Newsletter. Email addresses entered into the newsletter form
    • Recently viewed. A list of product IDs you have looked at

    What We Do Not Do

    • We do not send any of the above to a server
    • We do not use tracking pixels, cookies, or third-party analytics
    • We do not collect payment card details — none are ever requested
    • We do not create accounts or log you in
    • We do not share your data with any third party
    • We do not send emails, SMS, or push notifications

    Third-Party Services

    This storefront loads the following external resources:

    • Google Fonts — for typography (Cormorant Garamond and Inter)
    • Unsplash — for product and lifestyle imagery used as placeholders
    • Lucide — for the icon set used throughout the site

    These services may set their own cookies or log standard request metadata (such as your IP address) according to their own privacy policies. We do not control their behaviour and recommend reviewing their respective policies if this is a concern.

    Your Control Over Your Data

    Because everything is stored in your browser, you have full control:

    • Clearing your cart: Use the "Clear Bag" button on the cart page
    • Clearing your wishlist: Use the "Clear Wishlist" button on the wishlist page
    • Clearing everything: Clear your browser's site data for this domain. This removes all local storage
    • Using private browsing: All data is discarded the moment you close the tab

    Security

    There is no backend, so there is nothing to intercept. Your data never travels over the network from this storefront. This is a demo environment, and any real-world storefront would need proper infrastructure (HTTPS, a secure server, encrypted database storage, etc.) before handling personal information.

    Children's Privacy

    This storefront is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

    Changes To This Policy

    As this is a demo policy, it may be updated at any time without notice. In a real storefront, meaningful changes would normally be communicated via email or a prominent banner on the site.

    Contact

    For any privacy question, reach us via the Contact page.

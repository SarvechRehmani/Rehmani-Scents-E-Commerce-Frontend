Our Approach

Depth Without Volume

Masculine fragrance is often reduced to intensity. We take a different view: the most memorable men's scents are composed, not shouted. Bergamot and pepper open with clarity; cedar, vetiver and oud provide structure without weight.

Whether you reach for a fresh aquatic on a working day, a leather-tinged scent for the evening, or an oud-forward composition for celebration, this edit is built around restraint and longevity.

  • Woody Structure Cedar, vetiver and sandalwood for quiet depth.
  • Fresh Openings Citrus and aromatic herbs that settle cleanly.
  • Evening Warmth Amber, oud and leather for after-dark wear.

Wearing Guide

How To Wear

Apply to pulse points — the wrists, the base of the throat and behind the ears. Warmth from these areas helps the fragrance unfold slowly. Avoid rubbing the wrists together; it crushes the top notes and shortens the opening.
Fresh and aquatic fragrances read well in daylight and office environments. Woody, leather and oud-forward compositions are better suited to evening and cooler months, where their depth has room to develop without competing with bright light and open air.
Keep bottles away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity. A closed drawer or the original box works well. A properly stored fragrance will hold its composition for several years, though citrus-forward openings will soften faster than oud and amber bases.
Choose 50ml if you rotate between several fragrances or want to sample a direction before committing. Choose 100ml if the fragrance has become a daily signature and you know it will be used consistently.

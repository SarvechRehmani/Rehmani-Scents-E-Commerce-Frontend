Shipping Policy

How we dispatch orders, how long delivery takes, and what to expect when ordering within Pakistan.

Last updated: 1 December 2024 Demo content for a fictional storefront

On This Page

    Demo policy. This document is illustrative content for a fictional storefront. It does not represent a legally reviewed shipping policy and should be edited before any real-world use.

    Overview

    Rehmani Scents ships across Pakistan. Orders are packed and dispatched from our demo fulfilment location, with most orders leaving within one working day of payment confirmation.

    Delivery Coverage

    We deliver to addresses across all major cities and most smaller towns in Pakistan, including:

    • Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana
    • Lahore, Faisalabad, Multan, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Bahawalpur, Sargodha
    • Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Abbottabad
    • Quetta and surrounding areas

    For locations not listed above, delivery may be available through courier pickup points. If your city is not in the dropdown at checkout, choose Other and add the city name in the area field.

    Delivery Timelines

    Standard delivery is estimated as follows, counting from the day of dispatch:

    • Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi: 2–3 working days
    • Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Hyderabad, Sukkur: 3–4 working days
    • Smaller cities and rural areas: 4–5 working days

    These are estimated ranges and can be affected by public holidays, weather, or courier volume. Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays begin processing on the next working day.

    Delivery Charges

    Standard delivery is charged at a flat rate for orders below the free-shipping threshold. Orders above the threshold qualify for complimentary delivery.

    • Free shipping threshold: Visible in your bag before checkout
    • Standard rate: Applied automatically at checkout for qualifying orders
    • Cash on Delivery: No additional handling fee on this demo storefront

    The exact charge for your order is always visible in the order summary before you confirm checkout.

    Dispatch & Packaging

    Every fragrance is sealed, quality-checked and packed in protective packaging before dispatch. Fragrance bottles are sensitive to heat and light, so packaging is designed to protect the contents during transit.

    You will receive a demo confirmation page after placing your order. On this storefront, no email or SMS notification is sent and no courier is contacted.

    Order Tracking

    You can track your demo order at any time using the Order Tracker. Enter your order reference and the phone number used at checkout to see the progress of your order.

    The tracker reads orders saved in your browser only. It is not connected to any courier API or live logistics service.

    Delivery Issues

    If your order has not arrived within the estimated window, or if you receive a damaged parcel, please contact support within 48 hours of the expected delivery date.

    • Include your order reference and the phone number on the order
    • Attach photos if the parcel or item was damaged on arrival
    • Retain all original packaging until the issue is resolved

    Address Changes

    Because this storefront has no backend, orders cannot be modified once submitted. If you need to change an address in a real store, contact support as early as possible — before dispatch is usually enough time to update the details.

    Contact

    For any shipping-related question, reach us via the Contact page or WhatsApp.
